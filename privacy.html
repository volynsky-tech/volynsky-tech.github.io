Privacy policy — Hermes

Last updated: 6 October 2026.

Hermes is a private, single-user assistant. Its only user is its owner, who is also its operator.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, Hermes can access the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts data, only to carry out tasks the owner asks for.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and any retrieved data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to third parties, except as needed to process the owner's own requests with the AI model providers the owner has chosen.

Limited use

Hermes' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
