Hermes

Hermes is a private, single-user personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service: it is not offered to, and cannot be used by, anyone other than its owner.

With the owner's explicit consent, Hermes uses Google APIs (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) to read, search and draft on the owner's behalf. Sending email or changing calendar events requires the owner's explicit approval for each action.
